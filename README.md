EMBER PACT — a turn-based card duel against the Ashen Warden. You and the Warden both start with 30 life. Spend ember mana to play cards from a 30-card deck built from 15 original card designs across 4 houses, and turn the tide with 4 spells. Outplay the Warden's hand, guard your life total, and burn his to zero to win.

## How to play

- **Goal:** reduce the Ashen Warden's life from 30 to 0 before he does the same to you.
- **Mana:** each turn you gain ember mana. Every card costs ember — play what you can afford, and plan around what you save for next turn.
- **Cards:** your 30-card deck is built from 15 original designs across 4 houses. Draw, play creatures and attacks, and chain combos.
- **Spells:** 4 spells are in the deck — spend them at the right moment to swing a losing board.
- **Controls:** mouse and keyboard. Click a card to play it, end your turn when you're done, and watch the Warden answer.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome
```

The game is plain static files under `game/` — no build step, no network requests.
